The goal of the game is to survive as long as possible and explore as much territory as you can. The game world is made 
of blocks, which can be used for building. Players can choose between two world generation modes: a pre-built map of 
Hungary or a randomly generated world. The world is populated with living organisms, including animals, plants, and 
fungi—with certain animals capable of attacking the player. Dynamic weather is also implemented, meaning extreme temperatures 
can reduce the player's health.
