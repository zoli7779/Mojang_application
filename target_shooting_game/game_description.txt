The goal of the game is to hit as many targets as possible using various weapons, including a bow, crossbow, throwing stone, 
and sling. The more targets players hit, the higher their score.

The game features five difficulty levels: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Advanced, and Hard. Increasing the difficulty raises the 
total number of targets to hit.

A round ends when all targets are hit, or when the player runs out of ammunition with targets still remaining. Clearing all 
targets displays a "Congratulations!" victory message, while running out of ammo triggers a "Unfortunately, you failed to hit all targets" 
game over screen. A dedicated stats page displays the player's high score.
